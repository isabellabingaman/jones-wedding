Travel

Getting There

Temecula does not have its own commercial airport, but there are several convenient airports within about an hour’s drive of the city.

ONT

Ontario International Airport

Approximately 54 miles away · About a 55-minute drive

SNA

John Wayne Airport

Approximately 43 miles away · About a 50–60 minute drive

SAN

San Diego International Airport

Approximately 60 miles away · About a 60-minute drive, depending on traffic

We recommend renting a car or using a rideshare service to travel between the airport and Temecula.
